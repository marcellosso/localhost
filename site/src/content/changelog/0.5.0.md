- **Machines: another computer's Projects, from here.** Add a dev box, a home server or a cloud VM you reach with `ssh` in **Settings → Machines**: type a `Host` from `~/.ssh/config` (the field lists them) or `user@host:port`, and localhost connects before it keeps it. Its Projects show in the sidebar under its name, below this computer's, and you start, stop, restart and read their logs as you do here. They run on the Machine, with its configuration and Secrets, and are edited there. See [Machines](/docs/machines).
- **Open in Browser forwards the Port.** For a Machine's Service, Open in Browser first forwards its Port to the same port on this computer, through the SSH connection and bound to loopback only, so `http://localhost:5173` there is `http://localhost:5173` here. If that port is taken here, it says by whom rather than using another.
- **Over the SSH you already use.** localhost runs your system's `ssh`, so your keys, the SSH agent, `~/.ssh/config`, `ProxyJump` and Tailscale all apply, and it adds no sign-in and no port of its own. It never asks for a password, and never trusts a host key for you.
- **It says why it can't connect.** An untrusted or changed host key, a sign-in that needs a password, a host that isn't found or doesn't answer, localhost missing on the Machine or too old: each in plain words, with what to do next. A lost connection is made again on its own, and on a Mac as soon as it wakes; reconnecting never starts or stops a Service. See [When it can't connect](/docs/machines#when-it-cant-connect).
- **Quitting asks about Machines too.** Services you started from here on a Machine stop there once the connection ends, unless localhost is open on it, and quitting says so first.
- **An old background core says what to do.** When localhost was updated but an older version still runs in the background, often for a coding agent's `localhost mcp`, it now names that version and asks you to quit it and the agents using it, here and on a Machine.
- The first-run screen's three actions are stacked at one width, so they fit the column on every platform.
