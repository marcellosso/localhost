- **See the welcome and Get started checklist again.** On macOS, **Help › Show Welcome…** and **Help › Show Get Started Checklist** sit below Keyboard Shortcuts, and both are in the command palette. On Linux and Windows, the Keyboard Shortcuts button at the foot of the sidebar is now a **?** Help menu, with Keyboard Shortcuts and the same two actions; they're in the palette there as well.
- **Show Welcome…** opens the welcome even when you already have Projects, and doesn't change anything. **Show Get Started Checklist** brings the checklist back, and it stays until you close it with **×**, even if every row is already ticked.
- The quick-start now says how to see the welcome again.
