title: Dismiss a Failed Service
date: 2026-11-25
released: true
---

- **Dismiss a Failed Service.** A Failed Service now has **Dismiss** next to Start (also in its context menu, the command palette and on ⌘. / Ctrl+.). It marks the Service Stopped and clears the red marker from the row, the sidebar and the menu bar or tray. Its last Run and log are kept.
- **Stop All clears failures too.** Stop All is available while any Service of the Project has Failed, and leaves every Service Stopped, including ones that had Exited.
