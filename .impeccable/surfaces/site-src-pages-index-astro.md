# Landing: "The labelled panel"

Scope: site/src/pages/index.astro, chosen from prototype B with its "window first" hero (B2), and the site-wide primitives in global.css that every page now uses. Mode: Persuade. Audience: developers who keep terminal tabs open to run their projects, then coding-agent users. Action: download or brew install. Proof: the product demo only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: localhost is the labelled breaker panel for your dev machine: every Service a switch with its label, one main switch for the Project. Refuses the long scroll of equal "Fig" sections and the floating screenshot.

OWN-WORLD: powder-coated enamel panel (deep graphite in dark, warm light grey in light), engraved Barlow Condensed caps for labels and headlines, embossed label-maker tape strips for Keys and ports, toggle switches with a lit indicator in the state colours, screw-head corners, a circuit directory card as the page index. The real AppWindow mounts in the panel as its main meter.

STORY: Visitor reads what localhost is, watches the real window start a Project's Services in dependency order (and can drive it), then reads the circuit directory: commands, ports, environment, agents, local-first, platforms.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the engraved headline on the left; lede, Download, brew chip and the platforms line on the right; the live AppWindow mounted in a screwed bezel, full width, already in view below them. Mobile stacks headline, copy, then the window. The switchboard hero was tried and not chosen.

FORM: impeccable's pick (top-ranked grounded candidate, 1 of 7), seed:bd32e20dc8cf46b3. Raise from the seven-segment family: unlit indicators are drawn, not removed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
